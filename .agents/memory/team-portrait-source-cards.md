---
name: Team portrait source cards
description: Notes for regenerating and reviewing SinTech’s portrait crops.
---

The supplied team photos are promotional cards, not clean portrait originals; some include text or graphics close to the person. Keep the original files and use individually reviewed crops with a circular mask so nearby card copy does not leak into the site portraits.

**Why:** Repeated crop previews showed that a technically valid square crop could still leave text fragments or cut into faces.

**How to apply:** When a source image or crop changes, regenerate the contact sheet and visually inspect every portrait at the displayed size. If a clean crop is not possible, use the initials fallback rather than publish a crop with overlaid text.
